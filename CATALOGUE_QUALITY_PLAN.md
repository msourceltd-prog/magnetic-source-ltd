## Confirmed category corrections

The legacy **Bamboo Peg Set** entries do not belong in Kitchen & Dining and will not be retained there. The legacy **Insulated Lunch Pouch** entries do not belong in Personal Care and will not be retained there. The replacement catalogue uses distinct category-appropriate products instead of relocating repetitive placeholder entries.

## Product-quality rules

Each replacement record will use a distinct generic product name, short factual description, category-aligned SKU, pack format, GBP price, availability, and an appropriate product-oriented image. Variants will only be separate records when size, pack quantity, material, colour, capacity, format, or intended use materially differs. Images will use category-specific ecommerce/packshot-style sources and will not be reused across unrelated categories.
