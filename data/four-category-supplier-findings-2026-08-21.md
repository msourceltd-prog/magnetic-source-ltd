## Image Rule

Every shortlisted supplier detail page must provide a direct matching product image. The import will use a contained white-frame presentation so the full pack, bottle, box, object, or handle remains visible. Images that look cropped, zoomed excessively, or do not show a complete product will be rejected before import.
