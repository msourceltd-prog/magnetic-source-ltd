# Our Brands Gallery Research

The requested location is directly above the retained Magnetic Source footer. The supplied Gem Imports reference uses a light, simple brand-gallery field with a centred heading and a horizontal collection of brand marks.

The public Gem Imports homepage was consulted as the user-specified supplier reference. Image-search results confirmed a public Little Learners brand mark, a named brand that is explicitly present in the Magnetic Source catalogue. The catalogue audit identifies 54 explicitly named brands; the footer gallery must be limited to named brands actually represented in that catalogue and must not suggest brands for generic product ranges.

The gallery implementation will use a restrained, scrollable brand-name mark treatment for the strongest explicitly represented lines, avoiding any unverified logo or asset reuse while still clearly showing the represented brands.
