# UK Wholesale Research Notes

This research informs catalogue structure and presentation only. It must not be used to copy competitor descriptions, images, proprietary SKUs, stock levels, or unsupported brand/supplier claims.

## Category guardrails

The category research confirms that Household & Cleaning must remain cleaning, hygiene, paper, waste, and cleaning-accessory relevant; Personal Care should contain consumer toiletry and grooming lines; Kitchen & Dining should contain physical food-storage, cooking, tableware, and serving accessories; Medical & First Aid must avoid unsupported medical-device and treatment claims; Electrical Accessories must not claim safety, compliance, load ratings, or approvals without evidence; and Pets must not include veterinary medicines or medical claims.

## Catalogue decision

Because Magnetic Source has not supplied confirmed brand authorisations, EANs, supplier invoices, official product assets, or actual supplier price lists, the updated catalogue must retain **generic product names**, **internal SKUs**, **neutral supplier-photo placeholders**, and **clearly labelled indicative ex-VAT unit prices** pending owner-provided approved product data.
