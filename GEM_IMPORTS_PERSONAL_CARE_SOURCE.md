# Gem Imports Personal Care Source Record

**Owner-provided source:** https://www.gemimports.co.uk/dept/wholesale-personal-care_d0114.htm

## Public fields observed

The category page presents product name, supplier product code, unit price, carton quantity, carton price, VAT treatment, and public stock/pre-order text. The first page identifies **244 items** and provides a “Show all” route. Price text is primarily stated as **excl. VAT**, with some lines explicitly marked **zero VAT**.

## Import rules

Only source-listed Personal Care product names, codes, pack quantities, price values, and explicit VAT treatment will be transferred. The site will use the source’s **unit price** as the product price and preserve the stated VAT basis. Public stock counts and marketing/pre-order messages will not be copied as live availability; availability will remain confirmation-based unless an approved live inventory feed is provided. Supplier images remain neutral placeholders until image-use permission is confirmed.

## Completed strict import

The source listing contained **244** records. The strict category process retained **127** records assigned by the source to care, beauty, hair care, toiletries, dental, or shaving fields and explicitly excluded **117** lines assigned to other source areas such as seasonal textiles, sports and fitness, water bottles, weights, and charms. The website now uses the verified Gem Imports unit price, source-derived product name, supplier code prefixed as `GEM-`, and carton quantity for those 127 Personal Care entries.

Supplier availability statements and source images were not republished. The site retains “Availability to confirm” and the approved neutral image placeholder until a permitted live source or image authorization is available.
